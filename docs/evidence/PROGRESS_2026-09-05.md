## Implemented product scope / 已实现功能范围

The following capabilities are present in the current repository:

- exact Fabric client targets for Minecraft `1.21.11`, `26.1.2`, and `26.2`;
- Fabric Loader mod-graph collection with privacy filtering rather than raw
  local path upload;
- active resource-pack and shader-pack observation, scoped file hashing, and
  signed client snapshots after visible, connection-scoped consent;
- signed upload/protocol paths through Velocity and BungeeCord, with Paper and
  Folia backend support;
- independent server evidence adapters and correlation paths for behavioral
  detections, Grim, and Vulcan-compatible provider events;
- signed policy, admission, evidence review, federation, replay/expiry, and
  reversible current-connection disposition contracts;
- fail-closed scripts for version compatibility, release bundle identity,
  Matrix V4, Federation V5, Vulcan V3, Production Authority V4, and final
  readiness.

The current product boundary remains deliberately narrower than Tencent ACE:

- no kernel driver, persistent background agent, cross-process memory scanner,
  debugger/injection blocker, or DMA detector;
- no claim of public-server precision/recall or production kick/deny/ban
  effectiveness;
- client-reported mod/resource-pack facts remain low-authority evidence and do
  not independently authorize a high-impact action;
- Production Authority remains fail-closed and MONITOR-only until genuine
  externally signed production evidence is retained;
- automatic permanent bans are outside the v0.0.1 contract.

In short, the verified chain is client telemetry (mod list and active packs),
signed upload, server-side evidence correlation, and bounded disposition. It is
not a kernel anti-cheat.
